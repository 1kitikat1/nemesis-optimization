
Why I Created NEMESIS STEAM BOOSTER
I've been gaming on low-end PCs for years, and I know exactly how frustrating it is when your FPS drops in the middle of a crucial moment. The stuttering, the lag, the constant freezes — it ruins the experience completely.

The Problem
Most gamers can't afford high-end hardware. We're stuck with integrated graphics, old CPUs, and barely enough RAM to run modern games. And the worst part? Windows is bloated. There are dozens of background processes eating up resources, unnecessary services running, and settings that are optimized for "looks" rather than performance.

I spent months tweaking, testing, and finding every possible optimization to squeeze every last frame out of my system. And I realized — this knowledge should be available to everyone, not just those who can afford expensive hardware.

Why This Project Exists
I created NEMESIS STEAM BOOSTER for one simple reason: to help gamers like me get the most out of their PCs.

The FREE version includes:

RAM optimization

System tweaks

Game library management

DEV console

Basic FPS improvements

These are the essential tools that every gamer should have access to — regardless of their budget.

Here's the honest truth: I'm a student/developer with a dream. I want to save up for better hardware — a proper GPU, a faster CPU, more RAM — so I can continue developing and testing new optimizations. The ELITE and NEMESIS tiers exist to fund this project.
